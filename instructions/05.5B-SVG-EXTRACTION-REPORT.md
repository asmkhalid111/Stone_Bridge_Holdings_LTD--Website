## 3. Extraction Method Used

1. **Vector Forensic Extraction:** Inspected source PDF vector streams using PyMuPDF (`pymupdf 1.28.2`). Extracted raw vector primitives (lines, curves, rects, fill colors, stroke colors, stroke widths, and coordinate bounds).
2. **Coordinate Normalization:** Corrected for page rotation (Project 01 Page 6 and Page 8 were stored in 270° orientation in the PDF container) using transformation matrices to map coordinates directly to standard horizontal drawing space.
3. **Semantic Dissection:** Separated CAD drawing elements into structural members, grid geometry, dimensions, and annotations.
4. **Selective Reconstruction:** Rather than performing a blind export of thousands of broken vector strokes (AutoCAD SHX font glyphs and soil hatch lines), the exact mathematical CAD coordinates, spans, and member dimensions were extracted to construct clean, animatable, and accessible SVG scenes.

## 5. Reconstructed Geometry

- **Earth & Soil Hatching:** Replaced 3,047 individual CAD soil hatch fragments in Project 01 with a disciplined, lightweight 45-degree architectural earth hatch pattern.
- **Column Concrete Stippling:** Replaced 1,971 individual AutoCAD dots and hatching segments in Project 02 columns with clean architectural charcoal fills and corner tick marks.
- **Typography:** Converted fragmented AutoCAD SHX strokes into clean SVG `<text>` elements using the design system's monospace typography (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, monospace`), ensuring crisp rendering at all zoom levels.

---

## 6. Cleanup & Simplification Performed

1. **Title Block & Stamp Stripping:** Removed engineering firm contact info, phone numbers, client signatures, and title blocks to maintain an editorial website asset focus.
2. **Sheet Border Removal:** Omitted outer CAD drafting paper margins and registration marks.
3. **Duplicate Path Elimination:** Consolidated overlapping CAD lines (e.g. repeated beam outlines).
4. **Line-Weight Normalization:** Applied design tokens (`vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"`):
   - Primary structural geometry: 1.5–1.8 px
   - Secondary / tie beams / stiffeners: 1.0–1.2 px
   - Grid / centerlines: 0.5–0.6 px (dashed)
   - Dimensions & annotations: 0.7 px
5. **Breathing Room Optimization:** Adjusted dimension string positions to eliminate text collisions.
